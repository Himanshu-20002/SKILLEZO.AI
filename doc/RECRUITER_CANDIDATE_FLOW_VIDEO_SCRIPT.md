## 💡 Director's Recording Tips for Maximum Impact

1. **Dual Window Snap:** On Windows, press `Win + Left Arrow` on the Recruiter browser, and `Win + Right Arrow` on the Candidate browser during Scene 1. This creates a stunning 50/50 split that looks extremely modern.
2. **Audio Levels:** Speak with high energy. Treat this like an Apple or Stripe keynote announcement: clear diction, short punchy sentences.
3. **No Dead Air:** Transition smoothly between screens. If you need 1 second to click, keep talking through the action.
4. **Certificate Zoom:** When claiming the SHA-256 credential in Scene 6, momentarily zoom in or pause your mouse over `SKZ-CERT-...` — enterprise clients love cryptographic proof.
