## 📌 1. Executive Summary: How Skillezo Makes Money

SKILLEZO.AI is not just a  interview tool—it is an **all-in-one career acceleration toolkit** that guides job seekers from their initial resume draft to clearing their final corporate interview round.

We monetize across the entire candidate journey using a **"Freemium to Premium"** model:

1. **Free Tier acts as the magnet:** Job seekers join for free to build an ATS-friendly resume and experience a realistic AI mock interview. This brings thousands of organic users with zero ad spend.
2. **Candidates pay to win the job:** 
   - Those applying to jobs pay to unlock **Unlimited Resume Tailoring** that passes company ATS resume scanners.
   - Those with scheduled interviews pay for the **7-Day Sprint Pass** to practice role-specific interviews and unlock **Senior Model Answers**.
3. **Recruiters pay to cut screening time:** Companies spend 20+ hours a week reviewing fake or low-quality applications. They pay Skillezo monthly to access pre-verified candidates who have proven their technical skills in live AI tests.
4. **Universities pay to boost campus placements:** Engineering and business colleges buy annual student packages so entire batches are interview-ready before top companies visit campus.

## 💰 2. Profit Margins & Safe Cost Protection (The "Sweet Spot")

A common concern with AI products is unpredictable server bills. SKILLEZO is architected so running costs stay negligible:

* **Video and Voice are 100% Free to Run:** Video mirrors and speech recognition run directly in the candidate’s browser—**costing Skillezo ₹0 in streaming bandwidth**.
* **Resume Generation & ATS Scan Cost:** Tailoring a resume to match a specific job description costs **~₹0.35 ($0.004 USD)**.
* **Mock Interview AI Cost:** One full 15-minute mock interview costs **~₹1.30 ($0.015 USD)**.
* **The Profit Math:**
  * If a candidate buys a **₹199 Sprint Pass** and generates 10 tailored resumes + 5 mock interviews, our total cost is under **₹12**.
  * **Net Profit Margin: >93% on every paying user.**
* **Free User Safety Cap:** Free users get **1 Master Resume** and **1 Mock Interview per month**. This costs Skillezo **< ₹3 per free user/month**, making viral free growth completely safe.

### Stream 1: Candidates (Job Seekers, Students & Resume Builders)

Candidates use Skillezo for two major needs: **getting shortlisted (Resume/ATS)** and **clearing the interview (Mock Coach)**.

#### 🟢 1. Free Forever Plan — ₹0
* **Goal:** Maximum signups, word-of-mouth growth, habit formation.
* **Resume Features Included:**
  * 1 Master Resume Builder with industry-standard formatting.
  * Basic ATS Score Checker (scans 1 job description per week).
  * Standard PDF export.
* **Interview Features Included:**
  * 1 Full AI Mock Interview per month (Voice + Video mirror).
  * Overall Readiness Score (0-100) and performance badge.
* **What is Locked (The Teasers):**
  * Multi-job resume tailoring is locked (*"Tailor this resume for Amazon / Google job requirements"*).
  * Senior Engineer Model Answers are blurred in the interview report.
  * Deep speech fluency & filler-word diagnostics are locked.

#### ⚡ 2. The 7-Day "Job Hunt & Interview Sprint Pass" — ₹199 / $4.99 (Best Seller)
* **Target:** Candidates who are actively applying this week or have an upcoming interview round in 3–7 days.
* **No Subscription Fear:** One-time purchase, no auto-renewing credit card charges.
* **What they get for 7 Days:**
  * **Unlimited AI Resume Tailoring:** Customize their resume for 20+ different job postings with 1 click to beat company ATS filters.
  * **Unlimited AI Mock Interviews:** Practice technical and HR rounds across all 10 industry roles.
  * **All Senior Model Answers Unlocked:** Read the exact golden answers top candidates give.
  * **Instant ATS Keyword Gap Visualizer:** See exact missing skills before applying.

#### 👑 3. Skillezo PRO — ₹499 / month (or ₹3,999 / year)
* **Target:** Active career switchers and graduates applying to jobs continuously.
* **What they get:**
  * **Everything in the Sprint Pass on an ongoing monthly basis.**
  * **Hosted Developer Portfolio:** A personalized link (e.g. `skillezo.me/alex-dev`) showcasing their live projects, skills, and verified interview test scores.
  * **AI Cover Letter Generator:** 1-click tailored cover letters for every application.
  * **"Skillezo Verified Candidate" Badge:** Candidates with 80+ readiness scores get highlighted at the top of recruiter searches.

### Stream 2: Recruiters & Hiring Companies (B2B)

Recruiters receive 500+ AI-written resumes per job opening. 75% of applicants cannot answer basic technical questions on the phone.

* **Product 1: Pre-Vetted Talent Directory (₹2,999 – ₹7,999 / month)**
  * Recruiters search candidates by **Proven Readiness Score** (*e.g. "React developers with >80 score in live coding interviews"*).
  * Pay tokens or monthly subscription to unlock verified contact details and interview transcripts.
* **Product 2: "Skillezo Screen" — Automated Candidate Screening Link**
  * Company posts a job and includes a **Skillezo 15-minute screening link**.
  * Applicants complete the proctored AI interview. AI checks technical accuracy, flags red flags, and delivers a ranked shortlist to the recruiter.
  * **Pricing:** ₹99 per candidate screened, or ₹4,999/month for 50 candidates.

### Stream 3: Colleges & Universities (B2B2C Institutional)

Engineering and MBA colleges are judged by their **placement statistics**. They lack the staff to conduct 1-on-1 resume reviews and mock interviews for 1,000+ students.

* **"Skillezo Campus Hub" (₹300 – ₹500 per student / year billed to the college)**
  * Bulk resume building + ATS training for entire college batches.
  * Simulated placement interview drives before TCS, Infosys, Wipro, and startups arrive.
  * Placement Dean Dashboard showing batch-wise strengths and skill gaps.
