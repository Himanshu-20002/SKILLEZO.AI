# 🚀 SKILLEZO AI

> **AI-Powered Learning & Hiring Platform**

SKILLEZO AI is an intelligent career development platform designed to bridge the gap between **education and employment**. It leverages Artificial Intelligence to analyze resumes, identify skill gaps, recommend personalized learning paths, track career progress, and match users with relevant job opportunities.

---

## 🌟 Vision

To become the leading AI-powered career intelligence platform that transforms the journey from education to employment through personalized learning, skill-gap analysis, and intelligent job recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Mission

Our mission is to empower students and job seekers with AI-driven career guidance by providing:

- Personalized Skill Gap Analysis
- AI Career Coaching
- Intelligent Learning Recommendations
- Resume Intelligence
- Career Roadmaps
- Job Matching

# 📌 Problem Statement

Current career platforms focus on only one aspect of the hiring process—either learning, resume building, or job searching.

SKILLEZO AI brings everything together into one intelligent ecosystem where users can:

- Analyze resumes
- Discover missing skills
- Learn required technologies
- Build projects
- Track career progress
- Apply for relevant jobs
